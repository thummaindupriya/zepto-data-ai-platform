The classifier comparison should be interpreted using F1 and AUC together; the selected model by test-set F1 is Decision Tree (F1=0.7481, AUC=0.7904). The class-imbalance experiment compares the baseline, class-weighted, and SMOTE approaches, with recall and F1 showing how imbalance handling changes positive-class detection. The fare regression achieved R2=0.3975 and adjusted R2=0.3655; its residual analysis conclusion is: Residual spread increases toward higher predicted fares, providing evidence of heteroscedasticity. These results should be considered together with the EDA findings and the limitations of the Titanic dataset before applying the workflow to new data.
